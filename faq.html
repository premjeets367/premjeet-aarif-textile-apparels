Common Questions, Answered

What is Textile Industry?+

The textile industry covers the full chain of converting raw fibre — cotton, silk, wool or synthetic — into yarn, fabric and finished apparel through spinning, weaving, dyeing, and stitching processes.

What is Powerloom?+

A powerloom is a mechanized weaving machine that automates the process of interlacing yarn into fabric, producing cloth much faster and more uniformly than hand-operated looms.

Difference between Khadi and Powerloom?+

Khadi is hand-spun and hand-woven on traditional handlooms, giving it a distinct texture and breathability, while powerloom fabric is machine-woven at higher speed with more uniform output.

What is Yarn Twisting?+

Yarn twisting is the process of twisting fibres or filaments together to increase yarn strength, add texture, and prepare it for weaving or knitting.

What is Plastic to Cloth?+

Plastic to cloth refers to recycling PET plastic bottles into polyester fibre and yarn, which is then woven or knitted into usable textile fabric.

Which industries use Textile Products?+

Textile products are used across fashion and apparel, home furnishing, automotive interiors, sports goods, packaging, and industrial and technical applications.
