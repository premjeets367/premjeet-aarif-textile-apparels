Company Introduction

Our History

Textile & Apparels began as a small powerloom unit in 1990 and has since grown into a diversified manufacturing group spanning 12 production lines — from handspun khadi to export-grade denim and sports goods.

Every unit we run stays close to its craft roots while adopting the machinery and quality systems needed to compete on export markets.

MissionDeliver quality textile and apparel products through skilled craftsmanship and responsible manufacturing.
VisionTo be a leading, sustainable textile manufacturing group recognised across domestic and export markets.
ObjectivesExpand skilled employment, adopt eco-friendly processes, and maintain export-quality standards across all units.
Industry Overview

The Indian Textile Industry

One of the world's oldest and largest textile industries, and a major contributor to India's employment, exports and technology adoption.

Employment

The textile sector is among India's largest employment generators, especially in rural and semi-urban regions.

Exports

India is a top global exporter of yarn, fabric and finished garments across multiple categories.

Technology

Powerlooms, computerized knitting and digital printing are steadily modernizing traditional processes.

Future

Sustainable fibre, recycled textiles and automation are shaping the next phase of industry growth.

Our Journey

Timeline

1990

Founded as a single powerloom unit, weaving grey fabric for the local market.

2005

Expanded into khadi, knitwear and garment stitching, adding skilled tailoring capacity.

2020

Introduced denim, carpet, dari and recycled-fibre manufacturing lines.

2026

Operating 12 manufacturing units with export partnerships across multiple countries.
