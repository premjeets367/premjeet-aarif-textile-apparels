Welcome

Weaving India's Textile Growth, One Thread at a Time

India's textile industry is one of the oldest and largest in the world, contributing significantly to the nation's exports, GDP and rural employment. At Textile & Apparels, we operate across 12 manufacturing units — from powerlooms and khadi to denim and sports goods — combining traditional craftsmanship with modern technology.

Our production supports thousands of skilled workers and feeds both domestic and export markets, staying true to sustainable, quality-first manufacturing.
