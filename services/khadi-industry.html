Introduction

Khadi Industry

Our khadi unit supports local spinners and weavers producing hand-spun yarn and hand-woven cloth, preserving a centuries-old craft while meeting modern demand for sustainable fabric.

How It's Made

Manufacturing Process

  • 01
    Ginning and cleaning of raw cotton
  • 02
    Hand-spinning yarn on the charkha
  • 03
    Warping and sizing of hand-spun yarn
  • 04
    Hand-weaving on traditional handlooms
  • 05
    Washing, calendering and finishing
Equipment

Machines Used

  • Charkha (Spinning Wheel)
  • Handloom
  • Warping Drum
  • Calendering Unit
Advantages

Benefits

  • Supports rural livelihoods and artisan employment
  • Breathable, eco-friendly and biodegradable fabric
  • Distinct handmade texture valued in premium markets
  • Low carbon footprint manufacturing
Where It's Used

Applications

Khadi shirts and kurtasSarees and dupattasHome décor fabricInstitutional and gift khadi products
