Introduction

Carpet Manufacturing

Our carpet unit produces tufted and hand-knotted carpets, combining pile weaving techniques with backing and finishing processes for a premium floor covering.

How It's Made

Manufacturing Process

  • 01
    Design charting and yarn dyeing
  • 02
    Tufting or hand-knotting pile onto backing
  • 03
    Latex coating for pile lock
  • 04
    Shearing and pattern trimming
  • 05
    Binding, backing and final inspection
Equipment

Machines Used

  • Tufting Gun / Tufting Machine
  • Latex Coating Unit
  • Carpet Shearing Machine
  • Carpet Binding Machine
Advantages

Benefits

  • Rich pile texture and pattern precision
  • Durable backing for high-traffic use
  • Custom sizing for residential and commercial floors
  • Export-grade finishing standards
Where It's Used

Applications

Residential floor carpetsHotel and office carpetingPrayer and decorative rugsExport carpet collections
