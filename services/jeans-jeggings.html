Introduction

Jeans & Jeggings Manufacturing

Our denim unit handles the full jeans and jeggings production cycle, including fabric treatment, stitching, washing and finishing for a market-ready product.

How It's Made

Manufacturing Process

  • 01
    Denim fabric inspection and cutting
  • 02
    Stitching panels, pockets and waistband
  • 03
    Rivet and button fixing
  • 04
    Enzyme or stone washing for finish
  • 05
    Pressing, tagging and quality check
Equipment

Machines Used

  • Heavy-Duty Denim Sewing Machine
  • Rivet Fixing Machine
  • Industrial Washing Machine
  • Denim Finishing Press
Advantages

Benefits

  • Durable stitching suited for heavy denim
  • Range of wash finishes (light, dark, stone, acid)
  • Stretch fabric compatibility for jeggings
  • Consistent sizing across production batches
Where It's Used

Applications

Men's and women's jeansJeggings and stretch denim wearDenim jackets and shortsExport denim collections
