Introduction

Silk Saree Manufacturing

Our silk saree division weaves premium silk fabric with intricate zari borders and motifs, blending heritage weaving techniques with contemporary designs.

How It's Made

Manufacturing Process

  • 01
    Silk yarn degumming and dyeing
  • 02
    Warping and jacquard pattern setting
  • 03
    Weaving with zari border and pallu work
  • 04
    Motif and border finishing
  • 05
    Final wash, pressing and folding
Equipment

Machines Used

  • Jacquard Handloom / Powerloom
  • Silk Reeling Machine
  • Zari Twisting Unit
  • Saree Finishing Press
Advantages

Benefits

  • Rich lustre and drape unique to silk
  • Intricate zari and motif detailing
  • Wide design range from traditional to modern
  • Strong demand in bridal and festive markets
Where It's Used

Applications

Bridal and wedding sareesFestive and occasion wearExport silk saree collectionsBoutique and designer collaborations
