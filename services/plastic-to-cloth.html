Introduction

Plastic to Cloth Industry

Our recycling unit processes post-consumer plastic bottles into polyester staple fibre and yarn, turning waste plastic into functional, sustainable textile material.

How It's Made

Manufacturing Process

  • 01
    Collection and sorting of PET bottles
  • 02
    Washing, shredding and flaking
  • 03
    Melting and extrusion into filament
  • 04
    Spinning into polyester yarn
  • 05
    Weaving or knitting into recycled fabric
Equipment

Machines Used

  • Bottle Shredder
  • Extrusion Machine
  • Filament Spinning Unit
  • Recycled Yarn Winder
Advantages

Benefits

  • Reduces plastic waste and landfill load
  • Lower water and energy use versus virgin polyester
  • Durable, water-resistant fabric output
  • Meets growing demand for sustainable textiles
Where It's Used

Applications

Eco-friendly bags and totesNon-woven fabric for packagingSportswear and outdoor gear liningIndustrial geotextiles
